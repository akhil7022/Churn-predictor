    How it works From CSV to score

    Inside the forest

    A Random Forest is 100 decision trees voting. Each tree asks yes/no questions; the share of trees voting “churn” is the probability.

    STEP 01

    Clean

    clean_data.py drops empty rows, fixes types, recodes bad ages & genders. 25 logged fixes.

    STEP 02

    Encode

    Gender, tier and contract become 0/1 columns. 12 features total; CustomerID removed.

    STEP 03

    Train

    churn.py fits 100 trees, max depth 15, on an 80/20 stratified split.

    STEP 04

    Serve

    app.py (Flask) answers /predict and /chat for this interface.

    Read this before trusting a scoreThe training data has hard “cliffs” (e.g. every Monthly customer churned). The model learned them perfectly, but the separate test file doesn't follow the same rules — so treat scores as a ranking of risk patterns, not a guarantee.
